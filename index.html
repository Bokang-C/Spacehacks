01 / OUR MISSION

A universe of
possibilities

Spacehacks is a space-themed hackathon that brings together developers, designers, and innovators to create solutions that push the boundaries of technology and exploration. Our mission is to inspire creativity, foster collaboration, and ignite a passion for space and technology in participants from all backgrounds.
